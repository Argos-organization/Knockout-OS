# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Knockout OS to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project for everyone.

Contributors are expected to participate in good faith and to treat other people with respect, regardless of their background, experience level, identity, or role in the project.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and constructive.
* Welcoming different opinions and technical approaches.
* Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
* Focusing on the project and its technical goals.
* Helping other contributors learn and improve.
* Respecting the project's rules, licenses, and security requirements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
* Personal attacks or deliberately insulting other contributors.
* Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
* Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
* Deliberately introducing malicious code or security vulnerabilities.
* Threats or other behavior that makes participation unsafe.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers.

For security vulnerabilities, **do not report them publicly**. Use the process described in `SECURITY.md`.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Possible actions include:

* A warning.
* Removal of inappropriate content.
* Temporary restriction of participation.
* Removal of participation rights for serious or repeated violations.

Actions will be based on the circumstances and severity of the incident.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and activities related to Knockout OS, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and official project communication spaces.
